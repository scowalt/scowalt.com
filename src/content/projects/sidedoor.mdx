## Why I Built This

X, Reddit and Instagram all want you logged in, in their app, or both before they'll let you read anything. [Nitter](https://github.com/zedeus/nitter), [Redlib](https://github.com/redlib-org/redlib) and [Kittygram](https://codeberg.org/irelephant/kittygram) get around that, but the public instances are down or rate limited half the time. I got sick of clicking a link, landing on a dead instance, and hunting for one that worked, so I made the extension do the hunting.
