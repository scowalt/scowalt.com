## Why I Built This

X, Reddit and Instagram keep putting up login walls, which is super annoying. They also want you to consume as much content as possible, and it's insidious. They've all gotten rid of the feed you used to control and replaced it with one built to maximize engagement, at the expense of your emotional health and anything that actually enriches you. If someone shares something with me, I just want to see it, and that's it.

[Nitter](https://github.com/zedeus/nitter), [Redlib](https://github.com/redlib-org/redlib) and [Kittygram](https://codeberg.org/irelephant/kittygram) show you the post without a login or a feed trying to pull you in. Sidedoor sends links there automatically and picks an instance that's actually up.
