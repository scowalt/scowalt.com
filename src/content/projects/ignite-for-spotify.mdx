## Why I Built This

[Rocksmith](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rocksmith/plus) teaches you real guitar by having you play along to songs, and people have made over 150,000 custom songs for it on [CustomsForge](https://customsforge.com/). There's no shortage of songs. The hard part is finding ones I actually want to play, and the music I like is already in my Spotify playlists, so I wanted to start there. It's also my only side project that real people use.
