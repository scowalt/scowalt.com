## Why I Built This

[Majesty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majesty:_The_Fantasy_Kingdom_Sim) was one of my favorite games growing up, and the Royal Advisor is one of my favorite video game characters. I wanted to type anything and hear him say it.

I'm a perfectionist, so this was also practice at shipping something good enough. The voice isn't perfect and the setup is held together with duct tape, but it works, and I shipped it instead of tweaking it forever.

## How I Built It

I pulled the Advisor's voice lines out of the game files, cut them into single lines at the pauses, and transcribed them. That came to 339 clips, about 28 minutes of audio, and I trained a [GPT-SoVITS](https://github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS) model on it on my home GPU. Claude wrote most of the extraction and cleanup scripts. The page sends your text to GPT-SoVITS's own server on that same PC.
